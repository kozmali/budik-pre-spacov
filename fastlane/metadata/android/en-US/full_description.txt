This app was created with one clear purpose – to reliably wake up even heavy sleepers. While ringing, the screen is split into a large Snooze area (73 %) and a smaller Dismiss area (27 %) so you don't accidentally turn the alarm off while half-asleep.

If you keep snoozing and pass your Time Failsafe limit, an emergency beep starts and a 5-line rescue system protects you from turning the alarm off in half-sleep. Supports system sounds, synthesized melodies, local MP3 files, YouTube, and Spotify tracks, with bilingual Slovak and English interface.
